ABOUT UNO

ONE SHOT. ONE STANDARD.

Derived from the Latin word 'unus', meaning one, UNO represents the idea that you only need one paddle to do it all. It is the single, reliable force behind every shot — no switching, no second-guessing, just one consistent response.

The UNO Paddle combines a full-foam EPP core with a T700 raw carbon fiber face to create a powerful, yet controlled playing experience. Its construction is engineered to dampen harsh vibrations while giving players instant access to effortless power, deep spin, and a larger sweet spot when they need it.

From baseline drives to quick exchanges at the kitchen, UNO offers a solid, connected feel with less of the sharp impact — more comfort, more control, more confidence on every point.
